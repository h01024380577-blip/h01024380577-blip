## About Me

Hello, I'm **Jiwon Heo**, majoring in **AI Big Data & Management**  
(Convergence Double Major: **Data Science**) at **Kookmin University**.

I build **LLM applications and AI agents** that people actually keep using. My work centers on **RAG pipelines**, **multi-agent workflows**, and **MCP-based tool integration**, with a focus on making AI answers **grounded in evidence** rather than just plausible.

Beyond models, I care about shipping the whole product: I've taken services from idea to production — designing the AI pipeline, building the web app, deploying on AWS/Vercel, and improving it with real user data.
